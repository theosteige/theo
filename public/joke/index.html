A man walks into a bar, sits down, and pulls a tiny, one-foot-tall man and a miniature grand piano out of his bag. The tiny man sits down and begins beautifully playing a classical masterpiece.

The bartender is amazed and asks, "Wow, where did you get that?"

The man replies, "There is a genie in a bottle in the alleyway out back granting everyone one wish."

The bartender immediately runs out the back door. A few minutes later, he bursts back into the bar, slams the door shut, and is completely surrounded by millions of quacking ducks flooding the room.

Furious, the bartender yells to the man, "That genie is completely deaf! I asked him for a million bucks, and he gave me a million ducks!"

The man orders another drink, shakes his head, and replies:

"Do you really think I wished for a 12-inch pianist?"
