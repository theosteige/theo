Above are some of the 50+ timelapses from the week of 08/07/26 - 08/15/26 where I learned to play the piano. I compulsively bought this piano off of amazon and spent about a week learning to play it. I bought it because I wanted to prove to myself I was not musically illeterate. I had a good time remembering how to read sheet music, and getting the feel for the layout of the piano. I learned the a first minute or so of fur elise along with a few other songs and snippets. I noticed that I could only play to my fullest ability when my mind was clear, and that when any unrelated thoughts crossed my mind the song would fall apart. This made the act of playing (and learning) feel almost like meditating. I wanted to learn quickly, and the only way to do so was to have a clear mind focused only on the task at hand. I plan on finishing fur elise and I hope keep playing every once in a while as long as this piano still works.
